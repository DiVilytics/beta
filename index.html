DiVilytics | Disney Villainous Analytics

Archive, track, and analyze your Disney Villainous sessions. Every game you record joins a shared community database, turning one evening around the table into win rates, leaderboards, and per-villain stats for you and everyone you play with.

Acknowledgments

Disney Villainous is a board game by Ravensburger.

Villain and box artwork is the property of Disney and Ravensburger, sourced from the Disney Villainous Wiki.

The DiVilytics web client is open source. The code lives on GitHub, where you can report issues or suggest features. The server (database and sign-in) is hosted by Supabase.

Game log data comes from games recorded directly in the app by players, a daily sync with Villainous Italia's log, and an additional historical log for 2-player games from BoardGameGeek.
