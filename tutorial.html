Tutorial

How to record a game around the table, link it to every player's profile, and find your stats afterwards. It takes a couple of minutes per game, most of it while you're already playing.

Before you start

1
Open it on your phone DiVilytics is made to be used at the table: it works in any phone browser, nothing to install. For quicker access, use your browser's Add to Home Screen and it opens full screen, like an app.
2
Sign in Use Discord or Google, then pick a nickname. It must be unique: it's how other players and the leaderboard know you. You can browse everything without an account, but you need one to record or claim a game.
3
Pick an avatar Open your Account page (your avatar, top right). Choose one of the Photos, or design your own on Build: body, face, eyes, hair and more over a background colour. Random shuffles one for you, Apply saves it.
4
Mark your boxes Also on the Account page, tick the expansion boxes you own. Then the My boxes filter can limit random draws to villains you actually have.

Record a game

1
Open New Game Tap 🎲 in the top bar. Date and time are filled in for you; add the location if you like, it helps when browsing the log later. Open New Game
2
Pick the villains Set the number of players, then choose each villain from its menu, or tap 🎲 on a row to draw one at random (Draw empty and Draw all do every row at once). Open Draw pool to leave villains out, draw only one pace colour (Pace+ also allows the neighbouring colours), or use My boxes.
3
Set the play order Row 1 goes first. Tap Shuffle order to randomise it, or drag a row by its ⠿ handle. Remove a row with ❌.
4
Mark yourself Tap 👤 on the villain you're playing. The game can't start until you do.
5
Play Tap Start Game and the timer runs. Tap + when all players have finished a round and the first player is up again.

No timer, or a game played earlier? Skip Start Game, type the duration and rounds yourself and go to step 7.
6
Control it from the lock screen The timer keeps running with the screen locked, and the lock screen's media controls drive the game: Play and Pause start and pause the timer, Next and Previous add or remove a round. No need to unlock the phone every turn.
7
Declare the winner and save When someone wins, tap Pause Game: duration and rounds are filled in. Tap 👑 on the winner, then Save Game.
8
Show the QR code After saving, a QR code appears. Pass the phone around so the other players can scan it and claim their villain. Need it again later? On your Player page, tap Open on the game, then Share QR.

Claim your villain

1
Scan and claim Scanning the QR opens the game's claim page. Sign in, then tap Claim next to the villain you played. The game now shows up in your profile and stats.
2
Fix mistakes Claimed the wrong villain? Tap Release and claim the right one. Players of the game can also use Edit details to fix the location, duration or rounds, and Share QR to show the code again.

Explore the stats

🕒 Game Log Every recorded game, newest first. Filter by number of players, tap a location on a card to see only games played there, or use Included villains to hide games with villains you exclude. Open Game Log
🏆 Leaderboard Villains and players ranked by % Wins, # Wins or # Games, overall or by table size. The % Wins ranking only includes entries with at least 5 games, so one lucky win can't top it. Open Leaderboard
🎭 Villains Browse villains by box or by pace and check the monthly report. Open a villain for its objective, win rate by table size, rivalries (opponents faced in at least 5 games) and a link to its rules FAQ. Open Villains
👤 Player Your profile, or any player's via search: win rate, longest win streak (in gold while you're still on it), results per villain, who you play with most, achievements and game history. Open Player
📊 Charts Win rates, popularity, game length, play times and more, one chart at a time from the menu. Charts are interactive: tap a bar, dot or slice for its numbers and a link to it, and some can be zoomed or dragged to explore further. Open Charts
⚙️ Account Through your avatar, top right: pick a photo avatar or build your own piece by piece, mark your boxes, see your achievements (global, per box and per villain), change your nickname, link another sign-in (Discord or Google), download all your games as a CSV, or delete your profile.
